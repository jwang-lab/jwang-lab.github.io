### Overview
Shuang is a PhD candidate at Indiana University Bloomington, where her research focuses on developing computational methods for single-cell and spatial omics. Specifically, she works on discovering spatial cellular community motifs, recurring patterns of cell organization within tissues, and on integrating multi-omics data to build a more complete picture of cellular behavior and tissue architecture. Before joining IU, she completed her B.S. in Computer Science at Jilin University.
